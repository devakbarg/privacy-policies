Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Failsafe Alarm ("the App") is developed by Mag Studios. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We do not operate a server, and the App has no account, no login and no sign-up. Your alarms, schedules, shift rotations, alarm history, sounds and settings are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see any of it, and none of it is transmitted to us.

Your Alarm History

The App keeps a record of when each alarm rang, whether it was snoozed, and when it was stopped. This exists so you can tell the difference between an alarm that failed and one you dismissed in your sleep. It is written and read entirely on your device, is capped at a recent window rather than kept forever, and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Calendar Access (optional)

If you switch on "Check my calendar for early starts", the App reads your upcoming calendar events so it can offer to wake you earlier when your first event needs it. This reading happens on your device only. Your events are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, are not stored by the App, and are discarded as soon as the suggestion is produced. The App never writes to, edits or deletes anything in your calendar. The feature is off until you turn it on, and permission is requested at that moment, not at install.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display a single banner advertisement on the alarm list. AdMob may collect and use data to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers — the Advertising ID on Android, and the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS
  • IP address
  • Usage data related to ad serving

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before any tracking identifier is used, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised, and the App works exactly the same.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App presents a Google-certified consent form before any personalised advertising is served, and respects the choice you make there.

There is never an advertisement on the ring screen, the bedside clock or the alarm editor. You can also opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings at any time. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove the banner and unlock the paid features permanently. Purchase records are handled by Google Play on Android and by the Apple App Store on iOS, and are not stored by us.

Analytics

We do not operate any analytics service, and we receive no reports about how you use the App. Google AdMob collects usage and diagnostic data of its own in order to serve and measure advertising, as described above. That data is handled by Google, not by us.

Data Storage

All app data — alarms, rotations, alarm history, imported sounds, theme choice and your purchase entitlement — is stored locally on your device. It is not accessible to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarms (Android): Required so an alarm rings at the minute you set rather than whenever the system next allows.
  • Notifications (Android): Required because Android delivers the full-screen alarm as a notification. Without it, the alarm cannot cover your lock screen.
  • Alarms and timers (iOS): Required so an alarm can ring through silent mode and Focus, and take over your lock screen.
  • Start at boot (Android): Used to re-arm your alarms after the device restarts, so a reboot overnight does not silently cancel them.
  • Calendar (optional): Read-only, and only if you enable calendar suggestions. Never written to, never transmitted.
  • Internet: Required for displaying advertisements and verifying the in-app purchase. Your alarms work with no connection at all.
  • Vibration: Used so an alarm can vibrate as well as ring.
  • Tracking (iOS only): Requested through the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and used solely to personalise advertising. Declining does not limit any feature.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates with Google AdMob for advertising, and with Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS) for the optional one-time purchase. We do not share your alarms, history or calendar data with any third-party service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

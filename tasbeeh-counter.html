Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Tasbeeh Counter - Dhikr & Tasbih Tracker ("the App") is developed by Mag Studios. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

The App stores your tasbeeh counts, streak data, and preferences locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers — the Advertising ID on Android, and the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS
  • IP address
  • Usage data, such as which parts of the App you interact with
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before any tracking identifier is used, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised, and the App works exactly the same.

You can also opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings at any time. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We do not operate our own analytics service, and your dhikr counts, streaks and settings are never transmitted to us. However, Google AdMob collects usage and diagnostic data of its own in order to serve and measure advertising, as described above. This data is handled by Google, not by us.

Data Storage

All app data (counters, streaks, settings) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback when counting.
  • Notifications: Used for optional daily dhikr reminders, only when enabled by the user.
  • Internet / network access: Required for displaying advertisements.
  • Tracking (iOS only): Requested through the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and used solely to personalise advertising. Declining does not limit any feature.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Tasbeeh Counter - Dhikr & Tasbih Tracker ("the App") is developed by Mag Studios. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

The App stores your tasbeeh counts, streak data, and preferences locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Usage data

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics services. No usage data is transmitted from the App.

Data Storage

All app data (counters, streaks, settings) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback when counting.
  • Notifications: Used for optional daily dhikr reminders, only when enabled by the user.
  • Internet: Required for displaying advertisements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

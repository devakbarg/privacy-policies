Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Blockbeam ("the App"), a block puzzle game, is developed by Mag Studios. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

The App runs entirely on your device. Your game progress (best score, coins, games played), your settings (sound, music, haptics, selected theme, unlocked themes), your daily-reward streak, and your ads/review preferences are stored locally on your device only. We do not require an account, and we do not upload, transmit, or store any of your personal information on our servers.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads. AdMob may collect and use data to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Usage data related to ad serving

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App presents a Google-certified consent form (UMP) before serving ads, and you can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the App's privacy options. You can also opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase ($1.99 USD) to remove ads permanently, and an optional "watch an ad" reward that removes ads temporarily. Purchase records are handled by Google Play and are not stored by us.

Notifications

With your permission, the App schedules local reminder notifications on your device (for example, daily-reward and streak reminders). These notifications are generated on your device only — we do not operate a push-notification server and do not send your data anywhere to deliver them. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics services. No usage data is transmitted from the App.

Data Storage

All app data (game progress, coins, settings, daily streak, IAP entitlement) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Internet: Required for displaying advertisements and verifying the in-app purchase.
  • Notifications: Used to show optional local reminders, such as daily-reward and streak nudges.
  • Vibration: Used to provide light haptic feedback during gameplay.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates with Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play Billing for the optional one-time purchase. We do not share your personal data with any third-party service beyond what AdMob collects to serve ads, as described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
